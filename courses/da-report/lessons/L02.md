# Challenge 02: Structure before prose

**Gate:** self-attested · **Points:** 10

Decide the skeleton first: title, section order (`Data quality` →
`Findings` → `Appendix`), and what belongs in the machine-readable
`metrics` block. Prose fills structure — it never substitutes for it.

**Checkpoint:** your section plan exists on paper before you touch
`starter/report.py`. Self-attested; record via `python verify.py progress`.
