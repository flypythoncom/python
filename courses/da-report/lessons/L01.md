# Challenge 01: A report is a contract with the reader

**Gate:** self-attested · **Points:** 10

A report promises the reader: "these numbers come from this data."
Before writing code, list which numbers your report must carry —
`total_revenue`, `rows_clean`, `top_category`, `revenue_by_region` —
and where each one comes from in `results.json`.

**Checkpoint:** you can trace every required number to its source field.
Self-attested; record via `python verify.py`.
