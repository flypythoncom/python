# Challenge 05: Honest boundary

**Gate:** self-attested · **Points:** 10

The verifier checks files exist, are real PNGs, and that summary numbers
are right. It does **not** check that the chart is readable, honest in
scale, or well-designed — a misleading y-axis passes the suite.

Write one sentence you would attach to these charts stating exactly what
was and was not verified.

**Checkpoint:** you have that sentence written down. Self-attested;
record via `python verify.py`.
